INTERNAL BUSINESS PLAN

Learn. Build. Ship. A STEM student accelerator.

Exponential helps the next generation learn AI and build real robots and physical products. We start with a community and tutoring, and grow into a full platform that helps people go from idea to design, components, manufacturing and shipping.

01

THE BIG IDEA

Make it easy for anyone to build physical things with AI.

We combine AI tools, hands-on learning, a strong community, and real-world supply chain support so that more people (especially kids and beginners) can turn their ideas into robots, gadgets and real products.

05

INITIAL FOCUS: KIDS & BEGINNERS

We start with coaching and tutoring to help kids and beginners learn AI and build real robots.

  • How to use AI for robotics
  • How to work with electronics (Arduino, Raspberry Pi, etc.)
  • How to assemble components into a robot
  • Project-based learning
  • Simple, fun and hands-on
06

CONTENT STRATEGY

We create content to grow the community and attract more builders.

  • Break down what people are building on X, Reels, TikTok
  • Find what’s getting the most views
  • Follow similar topics and trends
  • Start with “vibe coding physical devices”
  • Show real builds, simple explanations and the process
